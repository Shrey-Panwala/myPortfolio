Research work

ACM MobiHoc '25 · Houston, TX

FedQ-Fraud: A Quantum-Enforced Federated Learning Framework for Financial Fraud Detection

Peer-reviewed conference paper · DOI: 10.1145/3704413.3765511

Co-authored and implemented a secure fraud-detection framework combining a 3-layer GRU, MOON-based federated learning, Bonawitz-style secure aggregation, and QKD evaluation. The system achieved 97.47% global accuracy across 4 non-IID clients while comparing BBM92 and MDI-QKD for secure model-weight transmission.

Result: 97.47% global accuracy Security: QKD + secure aggregation Evidence: ACM DOI and local PDF
